How to Choose the Right Service

Start with what the water is doing. Water spraying, a sewer smell or a loss of hot water is an emergency, and the emergency pages tell you what to shut off while a plumber is on the way. A slow drain, an old heater or a remodel is a planned job.

If a problem keeps coming back, think about the cause and not only the symptom. A drain that clogs repeatedly calls for a camera look and often jetting, and a leak that keeps returning may call for replacing the pipe. Our plumbers recommend the repair that fits the pipe and your budget.

Burlingame has a range of home ages, from older houses near the downtown to newer builds on the hills. That affects which services come up most. Older laterals need sewer camera inspection, aging heaters lead to water heater replacement and old supply lines lead to repiping.
