Why a Camera Look Beats Guessing

The lateral runs underground and out of sight, and the first sign of trouble is usually a backup. A camera moves that discovery to a time you choose. It finds roots, cracks, offset joints, bellies and old repairs before they turn into a flooded floor.

It also helps you buy or sell with fewer surprises. A buyer who knows the state of the lateral can negotiate, and a seller who knows can repair before listing.

What We Look For

Root intrusion

Roots at joints are the most common finding in older clay laterals.

Cracks and offsets

Joints that have shifted or pipe that has broken let in soil and water.

Bellies and sags

Low spots hold water and waste and cause repeat clogs.

Grease and scale

A coated wall narrows the pipe and shows when jetting is needed.

Burlingame Lateral Tests at Sale

What the city requires when a property sells

Burlingame requires a sewer lateral test when a property with buildings older than 25 years is sold or transferred. The test is witnessed by a city representative. A water test fills the lateral to at least 2 feet above the pipe at the city cleanout for 30 minutes and allows a loss under 4 gallons. An air test is used in hillside areas.

A video inspection is not accepted as the test. The test checks groundwater infiltration and not alignment, grade, materials or roots. Repairs must be completed and approved before title transfers, with one free retest and a certificate good for 10 years, or 25 years after a full replacement.

That is why we use our camera first. It tells you whether the lateral is likely to hold water, so a failed test does not delay your closing. If it needs work, we explain the path to a sewer line repair.

The sale-time rule sits in Chapter 15.12 of the Burlingame municipal code, and the repairs it can trigger follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Reading the Report

We note each defect by distance from the cleanout, so you can match it to the video. A single root at one joint is a maintenance item. Several offset joints or a collapsed section is a repair. A belly holding water may pass a city water test and still cause repeat clogs.

We keep the report plain. You get a description of what the camera saw, how serious each finding is and what we would do about it, and you decide what to do next.

If a defect might affect a sale, we talk through your options before you share the report. A repair completed before listing is often simpler than a repair negotiated at the closing table.
