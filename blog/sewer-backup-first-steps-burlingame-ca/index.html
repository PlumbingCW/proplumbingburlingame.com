Sewer Backup: What to Do in the First Hour

If sewage is backing up in your home, stop using water, keep people and pets away from the affected area, open windows for air and call a plumber. If the whole street may be affected, also report it to the city. Do not use chemical drain cleaners and do not run the washer or dishwasher.

The steps below cover safety, cleanup and the question of who is responsible in Burlingame.

Stop the Flow

Every fixture drains into the same line. If it is blocked, anything you flush or run adds to the flood. Stop using toilets, sinks, showers, the washing machine and the dishwasher until the line is cleared.

Tell everyone in the house. A child who flushes a toilet will not know the consequence, and a visitor will not either.

Safety first

Protect People and Property

Keep away from the sewage

Raw sewage carries bacteria and viruses. Keep children and pets out of the area.

Wear protection

If you must enter, wear waterproof boots and gloves and wash your hands carefully after.

Ventilate

Open windows and run fans if it is safe. Do not use a vacuum on sewage.

Turn off power if water reaches outlets

If sewage is near electrical equipment, switch off the breaker from a dry location.

Photograph the area before cleanup. This helps with insurance and gives the plumber useful information.

Is It the City or Your Lateral

Responsibility in Burlingame

The City of Burlingame maintains the sewer from the right-of-way cleanout out to the main. The lateral from the cleanout to your house belongs to you, and city crews cannot relieve a backup on private property.

If neighbors are backing up too, or the cleanout at the property line shows the main full, report it to Public Works. If only your home is affected, the problem is probably in your lateral and a plumber should clear it.

How a Plumber Clears the Line

Locate the access point

We use a cleanout, a vent or a pulled toilet to reach the line.

Cable or jet the blockage

We cut roots, break the clog and flush the line. For repeated problems we use hydro jetting.

Run a camera

We look for roots, cracks or a belly that caused the backup.

Explain and quote

We show you what we found and quote any repair before work.

The first call is usually sewer line cleaning. If the camera shows a broken pipe, the next step is a sewer line repair.

Cleanup and Insurance

Sewage contaminates porous materials such as carpet, drywall and insulation, and professional cleanup is often needed. Call your insurer early, because many policies exclude sewer backup unless you have a special endorsement.

Keep photographs, the plumber's report and receipts. We provide a written summary of what we found, which can help a claim.

What the Smell and the Water Tell You

A single slow drain with a mild odor usually points to a trap or a branch line. Sewage that rises at the lowest drain in the house, such as a basement or garage floor drain, a shower or a tub, points to the main line. The rising level shows the blockage is beyond that fixture.

Pay attention to what else happens. If flushing the toilet makes the tub bubble, or running the washer pushes water into a floor drain, the main line is carrying more than it can and the blockage sits downstream of both fixtures. That detail saves time on the phone and helps the plumber bring the right equipment.

If you can see the cleanout at the property line, look at it without opening it. A cap that is wet or a cleanout that is full tells the plumber the stoppage is in or past that point. Do not open a full cleanout yourself, because the pressure can release sewage quickly.

Why Burlingame Lines Back Up

Burlingame has many mature trees, and roots are the leading cause of lateral blockages in older neighborhoods. Clay pipe has joints every few feet, and a root entering a joint catches wipes, grease and paper until the line closes. Homes built before the mid-1960s were not required to have a cleanout at the right-of-way, which makes clearing the line slower.

Heavy rain also plays a part. When groundwater seeps into a cracked lateral, the extra flow strains the line, and roots grow into the moisture. A backup that appears each winter is a clue that the lateral is cracked, and a camera inspection will show where.

The city's sewer plant sits on Airport Boulevard, and the system is maintained by the city from the right-of-way cleanout to the main. Everything on your side is yours to maintain, which is why regular camera checks matter on older lines.

What to Tell the Dispatcher

Give the dispatcher a short, clear picture. Say which fixtures are affected, whether the water is clear or contains sewage, when it started and whether neighbors are affected too. Mention if you have a cleanout and whether the house was built before the mid-1960s.

These details let us bring the right cable, jetter and camera on the first trip. They also help us decide whether the problem sits on your lateral or in the public main, so you call the right party quickly.

When a Backwater Valve Is Expected

The California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), expects fixtures that sit below the next upstream manhole cover to be protected by a backwater valve. A home with a basement bath, a low garage drain or a sunken laundry area may fall in that group. Ask a plumber to check, since the valve must be installed with a permit.

Prevention

Preventing the Next Backup

  • Keep grease and wipes out of the drains.
  • Schedule a camera check if roots are a known issue.
  • Install a cleanout at the right-of-way if you have none.
  • Consider a backwater valve if your fixtures sit below the street level.
  • Do not plant fast-rooting trees over the lateral?
