San Mateo's Sewer Lateral Rule

What the city expects of the owner

San Mateo's municipal code, section 7.38.432, makes the property owner responsible for the lateral. The city requires inspection in several cases. These include a sale, a remodel or addition permit valued at $90,000 or more, a backup at a commercial or multifamily building that causes an overflow and a change in sewer class.

A recent inspection within five years or a full replacement within twenty-five years exempts the property, as do emergency repair permits. The amendment was adopted on February 3, 2020 and took effect on December 1, 2020. Public Works can confirm what applies to a specific address, and lateral work in the public right-of-way needs an encroachment permit.

San Mateo also runs a citywide cost sharing program for private laterals, which is worth asking Public Works about before a large repair. We start with a sewer camera inspection so you know what the lateral looks like, and we handle the repair through a sewer line repair when needed.
