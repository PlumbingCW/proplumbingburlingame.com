When to call

Water Heater Problems That Need a Same-Day Visit

A heater that is merely lukewarm can wait for the next available slot. A heater that is leaking, smells of gas or makes a hammering roar needs a plumber today. Water near the burner or the electrical panel makes any leak more serious.

Water pooling under the tank

A leak at the bottom often means the tank itself has corroded through, which cannot be patched.

Smell of gas near the heater

Leave the house, call 911, then call the gas utility. Do not flip switches or light anything.

Rumbling or popping from the tank

Sediment is trapped under the burner or element and the steel is overheating.

Hot water that runs out in minutes

A failed dip tube, element or thermostat can cut capacity without any visible leak.

If water is escaping, close the cold water valve above the heater and, on a gas model, turn the gas control to pilot or off. Then call us.

What We Check on a Burlingame Water Heater

Burlingame water is soft, with average hardness near 32 mg/L in the city's 2022 report. That is good news for heaters, since less scale builds on elements and the tank floor. It also means a failing unit here usually points to age, a worn anode rod or high incoming pressure, not hard-water damage.

Pressure is worth measuring. The city runs pressure-reducing valves and booster pumps in the hills, and a house in the flats can see a very different reading from one near Skyline Boulevard. High pressure shortens the life of the heater, the fill valve and every faucet, so we put a gauge on the hose bib before deciding what to repair.

  • Thermostat, heating elements or gas control valve.
  • Pilot assembly, thermocouple and burner on gas units.
  • Temperature and pressure relief valve and discharge line.
  • Anode rod condition and sediment in the tank.
  • Expansion tank and water pressure.
  • Earthquake strapping, required in California under Health and Safety Code section 19211.

Repair or Replace the Heater

A repair is the right call when the tank is sound and the failed part is a thermostat, element, valve or pilot component. Replacement is the right call when the tank leaks, the unit is well past its expected life, or the repair would cost more than a share of a new heater.

Repair or replace at a glance
SituationUsual path
Heater leaks from the tank bodyReplace. A tank cannot be patched. See water heater replacement.
Leak from a fitting, valve or relief lineRepair. The tank is intact.
No heat, tank is relatively newRepair the element, thermostat or gas control.
Repeated repairs on an old unitReplace, or consider a tankless unit.
Rust-colored hot water onlyCheck anode rod and tank. May be repairable.

A Note on Burlingame Water Heater Rules

Strapping, permits and the 2028 shift

California requires water heaters to be braced or strapped against earthquake movement, and a swap or relocation gets permitted through the city Building Division. Burlingame also waives its permit fee when a licensed contractor replaces a gas water heater or furnace with a heat pump.

Bay Area air rules are changing too. In 2026 the Bay Area Air District delayed its zero-NOx gas water heater standard to January 2028, and it applies when you buy a new heater, not to the one already working in your garage.

If you are weighing a switch to a heat pump model, ask the plumber about the tradeoffs on your visit and we will go through your electrical panel, garage space and hot water use together.

Prep list

While You Wait for the Plumber

Shut off the cold supply

The valve above the heater turns clockwise to close. This stops water feeding a leak.

Turn off power or gas control

Switch the breaker off for an electric unit, or set the gas dial to pilot or off.

Move stored items

Clear boxes and paint cans from around the base so the plumber can work.

Open one hot tap

If the tank is draining, an open tap upstairs prevents a vacuum and speeds the drain.
