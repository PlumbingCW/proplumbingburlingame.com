The neighborhood

About Lyon-Hoag

The Burlingame Historical Society includes Lyon and Hoag on its neighborhood tour, and a local real estate guide says it was created in 1896 as the city's first subdivision, on land the Howard family had used as a dairy. We could not find an official map, so we describe it by its closeness to downtown Burlingame Avenue and Washington Park.

Real estate guides describe smaller, cozy homes, some on larger lots, with newer construction appearing more often in recent years. That mix of old and new is the useful point for plumbing.

Drains and Laterals Near Downtown

Homes close to downtown are served by lines that run to the city sewer in the street, and the owner is responsible for the pipe from the house to the right-of-way cleanout. When a drain backs up in an older home, the question is whether the stoppage is in a fixture branch or in the lateral.

A drain cleaning visit clears the immediate stoppage. If it returns, a camera look through sewer inspection shows whether roots, a belly in the pipe or a broken joint is the cause.

If You Are Buying or Selling

The Chapter 15.12 lateral test

Burlingame requires a sewer lateral test at sale for buildings older than 25 years. The test holds water two feet above the lateral for 30 minutes, and the loss must be under 4 gallons.

A video inspection is not accepted as the test, and the seller gets one free retest. Any repair follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). A passing certificate is valid for 10 years, or 25 years after a full replacement. Public Works handles the test fee and any encroachment permit.

A camera inspection before listing is not the legal test, but it tells you what to expect. Our sewer inspection page explains what the video shows.

Newer Rebuilds Beside Older Houses

When a rebuilt home sits beside an original one, the old lateral may still serve the new house. A rebuild is a good time to ask whether the lateral was replaced or only tied in, and a sewer line repair plan is easier to schedule before the yard is finished.
