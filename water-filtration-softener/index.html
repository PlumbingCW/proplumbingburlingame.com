Water Filtration and Softener in Burlingame, CA

Burlingame's water is already very soft, so most households do not need a water softener. What many want is better taste, and a filter or a reverse osmosis system addresses that.

What Is in Burlingame Water

Burlingame buys all its water from the San Francisco Public Utilities Commission, mostly from Hetch Hetchy snowmelt. The city's 2022 report lists hardness from 9.1 to 49 mg/L with an average of 32, which is well under 2 grains per gallon. Total dissolved solids average 61 ppm.

The water is treated with chloramine and UV. pH averages 9.2. The city notes it is among the cleanest supplies in the country, and your tap meets state and federal standards.

Do You Need a Softener

Almost never. A softener removes hardness minerals, and Burlingame water has very little. Installing one adds salt, a drain line and maintenance for little benefit.

If your water ever tests hard at the tap, perhaps because of a well or a different source, we can test and discuss it. Most Burlingame homes are better served by a taste filter.

Taste, Chloramine and Your Tap

San Francisco Public Utilities Commission water arrives in Burlingame disinfected with chloramine, and some people notice a swimming-pool taste or smell. Chloramine is a stable disinfectant and stays in the water longer than plain chlorine, which is why a carbon filter that handles chloramine matters. Not every carbon cartridge does, so we choose one that lists it.

Aquarium and kidney dialysis users need special treatment of chloramine, and a filter is not a substitute for guidance from your doctor or fish supplier. If that applies to you, mention it when you call.

Reverse Osmosis in Detail

A reverse osmosis unit pushes water through a membrane that removes most dissolved solids, and it stores the clean water in a small tank under the sink. It supplies a dedicated faucet, and it can feed a refrigerator ice maker and water dispenser.

Because Burlingame water is already low in dissolved solids, RO is a taste choice more than a necessity. It removes the last of the minerals as well, which some people like and some find flat. A remineralizing stage can add a little back.

The unit sends a portion of the water to the drain, so it needs a drain saddle. We install an air gap or a high loop as required, and we mount the unit so you can reach the cartridges.

Whole-House or Under-Sink

A whole-house filter treats every tap and shower. It makes sense for households sensitive to chloramine in bathing water or those with dry skin issues, and it needs proper sizing so flow stays strong. An under-sink unit treats just the kitchen tap and costs less to own.

For most Burlingame homes, an under-sink carbon filter or an RO unit does the job. We help you decide with a short conversation about what you want to change, and we do not push a whole-house system that you will not notice.
