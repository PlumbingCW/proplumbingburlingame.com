When a Clogged Drain Cannot Wait

A slow sink can wait for a daytime visit. A drain that is fully blocked, or one that pushes water back into a second fixture, cannot. When the waste has nowhere to go, it finds the lowest opening in the house, usually a shower or floor drain.

Two fixtures back up together

A tub that gurgles or fills when you run the washer points to a blockage in the shared line, not in one trap.

Water or sewage at a floor drain

Anything rising at the lowest drain in the house means the main line is blocked or full. Stop using water.

Toilet will not clear after plunging

If a plunger fails and the bowl rises when another fixture drains, the clog sits past the toilet trap.

Foul smell with slow drains

Sewer odor along with slow drainage often signals buildup in the main line, not just a dirty trap.

If any of these match, stop running water, keep children and pets away from standing water, and call us. Clearing the line early is far cheaper than cleaning a flooded room.

Why Burlingame Drains Clog

Burlingame water is very soft. The city's 2022 water quality report lists average hardness at 32 mg/L, so mineral scale rarely narrows the pipes the way it does in hard-water towns. That shifts the usual causes toward grease in kitchen lines, hair and soap in bathrooms, wipes in toilets and roots in the yard.

Roots matter most in older neighborhoods. Homes built before the mid-1960s were not required to have a cleanout at the right-of-way, and many still have clay laterals with joints that roots can enter. A line that backs up every spring or after heavy rain usually has a root problem, which is a job for the sewer line cleaning crew rather than a simple sink clearing.

Kitchen lines in restaurants and busy homes near Burlingame Avenue pick up grease that hardens inside the pipe. A cable opens a hole through it, but jetting is what removes the coating, so we recommend hydro jetting when a line keeps closing back up.

How We Clear a Drain, Step by Step

Find where it stops

We ask which fixtures are affected and run water to learn whether the block is in a branch or the main line.

Open the access point

A cleanout, a removed trap or a roof vent gives the cable a path. If there is no cleanout, we pull a toilet or work through a vent.

Cable the blockage

A motorized auger breaks through the clog and retrieves what it can. We run water to confirm full flow.

Camera or jet if needed

If the line closes again or the cable meets a hard stop, a camera shows why before we recommend the next step.

You see the problem on the camera screen when we use one, so the next step is your decision with the facts in front of you.

Who Maintains What in Burlingame

City sewer versus your lateral

The City of Burlingame maintains the public sewer from the right-of-way cleanout out to the main. The pipe from that point back to your house belongs to you, and city crews cannot relieve a backup on private property.

If your whole street is backing up, tell the city. If only your house is affected, the blockage is almost always on your side and we can clear it.

When a camera shows a cracked or offset section, we explain your options and the path to a sewer line repair.
