The neighborhood

About Mills Estate

Mills Estate is named on the Burlingame Historical Society tour, and local guides place it up by Interstate 280, just south of Trousdale, with Franklin Elementary nearby and Burlingame Plaza at the bottom of Trousdale. One guide calls it a more suburban, planned neighborhood, and another says homes are generally 50 to 60 years old with many one-level ranches.

Those are real estate descriptions rather than official figures, and we did not find an official boundary map. We treat them as a guide to what you might expect, not as a guarantee about your house.

Hills, Pressure and Water Supply

Burlingame uses booster pumps and pressure-reducing valves in areas above 120 feet in elevation, so supply pressure can differ between a street near the bay and one up the hill. We do not know which Mills Estate streets sit above that line, and the city can tell you about yours. Our work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

If your pressure is very high or very low, a gauge on a hose bib is the quick check. High pressure stresses fixtures and the water heater, and a failing pressure-reducing valve can cause leaks. A leak detection visit finds hidden water, and water line repair covers the pipe from the meter to the house.

Sloped Lots and Sewer Testing

Hillside lateral rules

Under Chapter 15.12, buildings older than 25 years need a lateral test at sale, and the city uses an air test in hillside areas in place of the water test. A video inspection is not accepted.

If you are selling a Mills Estate home, ask Public Works which test applies to your lot. A sewer inspection before listing shows the pipe condition.

Sloped lots can also mean a longer, steeper run from the house to the street. A drain cleaning visit clears a stoppage, and a camera look tells you whether it is likely to return.

Water Heaters and Strapping

Replacing a water heater in Burlingame needs a plumbing permit from the Building Division, and California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires the heater to be strapped. We handle the permit and strapping when we replace a water heater.
